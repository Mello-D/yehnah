HOME shows the selected track's robot walking a city street at dusk and by night (the device's firmware/src/ui_road.c, ported), its 16 steps the road's centre-line dashes. All twenty robots are below; click one to put it on the selected track.

Dancea new move on every beat, picked at random from sixteen: arms down, out, up, waving, on the hips or pointing; leaning, nodding, a side step, a foot up, knees bent or bent wide. On the off-beat it dips at the knees. Soundit hops and lights up on its notes, and its chest moves with the sound (the record spins, the tape turns, the drawbars rise, the static fizzes...). Scenethe street goes by a pixel a 1/16 (EAT, SLEEP, the club's PARTY and its visualiser, REPEAT), the towers behind at a half and a quarter of its speed; dusk turns to night and back; the neon pulses on the beat; the wet road mirrors it all. On a note, one time in ten, the robot slides to another of five places; back to the middle when its notes stop. Stepsthe selected track's 16 steps on the road's centre line, moving with it: a note in its robot's colour, an empty step white, the first step in the accent, the playhead bright white. Costeach robot stored in parts (head, body, arm, leg, foot), ~150–300 B compressed; the moves are a few bytes each.
