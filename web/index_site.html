A development release (v0.1.0). This is custom firmware: install it at your own risk. Your music is backed up before the installer writes anything, and the way back to the official firmware is one click.

What it does

Everything is synthesised in real time: there are no samples. It comes with a web installer, a web editor and an emulator that runs the firmware itself in your browser.

25 synthesis engines

Minimoog, Juno, FM, wavetable, strings, guitar, Omnichord, voices and animals. Any engine on any track.

Chords on one finger

Play an Omnichord or a guitar with single keys. Strum on hold, tunings and shapes.

Ideas that write for you

IDEAS throws a pattern, a sound, an LFO, an effect or an ambient texture at the track you are on, heard at once.

A real sequencer

64 steps a track with chords, parameter locks, ratchets, probability and micro timing. Songs of sections and scenes.

Play with the sound

SWARM, TRACE and XYZ move four dots across the screen and the sound moves with them. Capture the good ones.

Effects and MIDI

Drive, phaser, delay, six reverbs, chorus, slicer, DJ filter and punch-in effects. USB MIDI and stereo audio, TRS in, clock.

Try it now

The emulator is the firmware itself, compiled to WebAssembly, drawn as an FM-1 you play with the mouse, a touch screen or the computer keyboard. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your computer.

Install

Chrome or Edge on a computer, and a USB cable straight to the FM-1.

  1. Back up first. The installer saves a complete backup of the unit before it writes.
  2. Coming from another custom firmware? Return to official V15 first: writing Yehnah straight over another custom firmware has bricked an FM-1. The installer checks the unit and writes only over official V15 or over Yehnah.
  3. Open the installer, connect the FM-1 and follow it. The same page takes the unit back to official V15.
  4. If it crashes, freezes or clicks, hold REC while switching on (one core) and report what GLO > SYSTEM > ABOUT says.
